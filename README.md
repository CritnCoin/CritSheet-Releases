# CritSheet

D&D 5e character sheets, a character builder, a compendium, DM tools and a virtual table, for our group.

## Install

1. Download **CritSheetLauncher.exe** from the [latest release](../../releases/latest).
2. Run it. Windows may say *"Windows protected your PC"* (the launcher isn't signed with a paid certificate): click **More info**, then **Run anyway**.
3. The launcher installs CritSheet, puts a shortcut on your Desktop and in the Start menu, and starts it when you click **Play**.

From then on, open CritSheet from its shortcut: it updates itself whenever a new version is out.

To remove it: Windows Settings > Apps > Installed apps > CritSheet > Uninstall. Your characters and campaigns are kept.

## Books

CritSheet comes with the free SRD 5.1. To use your own books, point it at your copy of the books' data in Settings > Books.
